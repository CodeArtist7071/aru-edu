# User Panel: Comprehensive Feature Manifest

This document provides a detailed breakdown of every functional component and tool within the ARU.EDU User Panel.

## 1. Ecological Dashboard (`/user/dashboard`)
The primary mission control for your preparation.
- **Namaskar Greeting**: A personalized welcome that displays your current subject completion percentage and relative ranking.
- **Streak Tracker**: Monitors consecutive days of engagement to maintain momentum.
- **Daily Goal Progress**: A visual bar tracking hours spent vs. scheduled goals for the day.
- **Exam Registry**: Quick-access cards for your targeted exams, allowing you to jump directly into syllabus discovery.
- **Task Manifest**: A floating action button (FAB) that tracks remaining daily rituals.

## 2. Growth Analysis (`/user/performance`)
Deep-dive data visualization for syllabus mastery.
- **Growth Chart**: A temporal visualization of your accuracy trajectory across Practice and Mock test modes.
- **Subject Mastery**: Percentage-based breakdown of your performance in individual domain areas (e.g., General Studies, Aptitude).
- **Question Distribution**: A granular view of Correct, Incorrect, and Skipped questions.
- **Cognitive Insights**: AI-driven identification of "Strong Chapters" (80%+ accuracy) and "Weak Chapters" (<50% accuracy).
- **Soil Enrichment**: Tactical recommendations for areas requiring immediate focus.

## 3. Simulation Lab (`/user/mock-tests`)
High-fidelity testing environment.
- **Adaptive Selection**: Questions are picked based on your past performance to focus on weak points.
- **Difficulty Modes**: Toggle between Easy, Moderate, and Hard ratios for the test generator.
- **Environment Proctoring**: Immersive header manifests with a "Tempo Reset" (timer) and "Submit" triggers.
- **Preference Configurator**: Modular slide-in panel to set question count and time limits before starting.

## 4. Strategic Planner (`/user/plan-study`)
The "Exam Rituals" management system.
- **Routine Grid**: A calendar-like interface for scheduling study sessions.
- **Quick Schedule Edit**: Ability to rapidly adjust start times and dates for specific rituals via modal.
- **Progress Toggling**: One-click marking of rituals as completed, which immediately updates the global streak and progress metrics.

## 5. Achievement Manifest (`/user/results`)
Historical record and status tracking.
- **Log Manifest**: A chronological list of all past attempts.
- **Detailed Result Analysis**: Breakdown of marks, accuracy, and section-wise performance for finished tests.
- **Social Leaderboard**: See how you rank against the broader ecosystem of aspirants.

## 6. Global Ecosystem Utilities
System-wide tools accessible from any panel.
- **Blue Light Shield**: Eye protection filter for nocturnal sessions.
- **Botanical Theme engine**: Seamless transition between dark and light modes.
- **PWA Manifest**: Full app installation support for mobile and offline rituals.
- **Action Center**: (In-development) Quick-access hub for notifications and system alerts.
